# Cover Letter Template — AI Engineer Role

## Formula
Paragraph 1: Role + company-specific reason (2 sentences)
Paragraph 2: Most relevant project with specific metric (3 sentences)
Paragraph 3: What you would build for them in 30 days (2 sentences)
Closing: Direct ask

## Template

Dear [Hiring Manager],

I am applying for the AI Engineer role at [Company]. Your work on
[specific product from their website] is directly relevant to what
I have been building for the past 83 days.

I recently built an end-to-end RAG support bot using LangChain,
ChromaDB, and Anthropic Claude — deployed with FastAPI, authenticated
with API keys, and monitored with LangSmith. It achieves 0.78 overlap
score on structured evaluation and handles out-of-scope queries
gracefully. The full code and architecture are on GitHub.

In my first 30 days at [Company], I would focus on [specific problem
from their job description] by implementing [approach I have built].
I have working code for this pattern already.

I would love to show you a live demo.
GitHub: github.com/aryaraut13

Arya Raut

## Customization checklist per application
[ ] Read the job description 3 times
[ ] Find one specific product or feature to mention in paragraph 1
[ ] Match paragraph 2 project to their tech stack
[ ] Write paragraph 3 based on their actual problem
[ ] Remove all generic phrases like "passionate about" or "team player"
[ ] Keep total length under 250 words
